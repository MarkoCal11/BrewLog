BrewLog – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

BrewLog is a personal portfolio project for logging coffee brews, beans and cafés.

What data is collected

  • If you sign in with Google: your name and email address.
  • If you register directly: your username, email and a hashed password.
  • Content you add yourself: brews, beans, cafés and uploaded photos.

How it is used

Data is used only to run the app and show you your own logbook. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Cookies

BrewLog only uses cookies needed for signing in. No tracking or analytics cookies are used.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored on a server in the EU. To have your account and data deleted, email marko@markocaleta.com.
